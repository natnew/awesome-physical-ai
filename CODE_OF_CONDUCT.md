# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Expectations

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive. Critique ideas rather than people.
- Welcome questions, differing perspectives, and good-faith feedback.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern or participates in a review.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately by email:

**`opensourceresporting.ai@proton.me`**

Include what happened, relevant links or evidence, and any immediate concerns. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports and share information only as needed to investigate and respond. Anyone with a conflict of interest should recuse themselves from handling the report.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and recurrence, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Requests to reconsider a decision may be sent to the same reporting address with relevant context.
